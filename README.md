# Bible Stories

**Bible Stories** is a free Bible reading app for Windows: one Bible story a night until you've read the whole Bible. It works **offline**, reads each story **out loud**, and shows classic engravings by Gustave Doré and Julius Schnorr von Carolsfeld right where each scene happens in the text.

- **The whole Bible in 440 nights**, in order, from Genesis to Revelation. Each night has a story title and a date.
- **World English Bible**: a modern English Christian translation of the 66 books, with God's name shown as "the LORD" like most English Bibles.
- **Pictures that follow the story**: 319 public-domain engravings placed at the verse they show.
- **Listen**: reads the story aloud with your computer's voices. Pick the speed and the voice.
- **Checkmarks**: mark each night you've read and see your progress.
- **Ask about the story**: a chat box answers your questions right in the app, faithful to Scripture with chapter and verse. It has over 1,100 written answers and works offline, with no account needed.
- **Tonight's story in brief**: a short plain-English summary at the top of every night.
- **Updates itself**: each time it opens, it checks here for a newer version. Your checkmarks and saved questions are kept.

## Install (Windows, no admin rights needed)

1. Download **[Bible-Stories-Installer.zip](Bible-Stories-Installer.zip)**.
2. Right-click it, choose **Extract All**, and open the folder.
3. Double-click **Install Bible Stories** while you're online. If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
4. Open **Bible Stories** from your desktop or Start menu.
